## Mix correction and shared UI input

The previous action levels (0.055–0.11) and music levels (0.028–0.035) were too quiet in the owner's Studio test. `HeistAudioConfig.luau` now sets pickup 0.48, displayed 0.46, stored 0.36, first dollar 0.42, equip 0.38, neutral UI click 0.28, and music 0.11–0.12 before player settings. `ExhibitSoundConfig.luau` raises local passive exhibit clips to 0.18–0.24 and door opening to 0.38. Phantom Glow's spatial aura is 0.10. The existing travel warp was already 0.8 and was not raised. These are starting mix values, not a human-approved loudness target; phone speaker/headphone checks remain mandatory.

`UiFeedback.luau` now binds every `GuiButton` in `PlayerGui`, including later-created buttons, in both places. A neutral click plays on mouse/touch press (with `Activated` as keyboard/gamepad fallback) using clip `226892749` at 0.28 with an 0.08-second throttle and a short 0.96→1 button scale on normal effects. The scale is skipped in reduced-motion or low-effects mode. A click confirms input only; it never claims a purchase, equip, delivery or travel succeeded. The clip was supplied by the owner and loaded in both Studio places.
